What it does

Everything between "a pile of tasks" and a board that keeps up with you.

Built to get out of your way — sign in once, then just drag cards.

01

Multiple boards

Create as many boards as you need. Tabs auto-paginate when they overflow — double-click a tab to rename it.

02

4-zone Kanban

Archive → To Do → In Progress → Done. Drag cards between columns, or reorder within a column.

03

Inline editing

Double-click — or double-tap on mobile — any card to edit its title right where it sits.

04

Owner & due dates

Each card tracks an assignee and optional due date. Overdue turns red, due within 3 days turns orange.

05

Owner filter

Quick-filter chips above the board — tap a name to see only that person's cards, on any board.

06

Confetti on Done

Every card you drag to Done gets a small celebration. A tiny reward, every single time.

No middleman

Taskboards doesn't keep your data. Google does.

There's no Taskboards database and no backend storing your boards — everything lives in a private folder inside your own Google Drive.

Privacy 01

No server of ours

Board data goes straight from your browser to your Google Drive. We never see it, never store a copy, and could never leak what we don't have.

Privacy 02

Yours to revoke

Remove Taskboards' access any time from your Google Account permissions, and every board it ever wrote goes with it — because it was never ours.
